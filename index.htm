Tony Huiquan Zhang

Associate Professor of Sociology

University of Macau

I am an Associate Professor of Sociology at the University of Macau. My research focuses on social and political psychology, comparative politics (especially China and the Asia-Pacific), leadership and organization, the Internet and social media, inequalities, and diversities.

我是张汇泉，现任澳门大学社会学系副教授。我的研究兴趣包括社会与政治心理学、政治文化与公众意见、比较研究（中国及亚太）、互联网与社交媒体、不平等与多样性等领域。

Alongside my academic work, I write lyrics and songs for Mandarin pop music. I am also working on a book about writing Mandarin pop lyrics.

除了社会科学研究，我也是一名国语流行音乐作词人，正在写一本有关华语流行歌词的中文书稿（暂名《国语流行歌词创作随谈》）。

Research

My work examines social and political attitudes, leadership, communication, and inequality in comparative perspective, with particular attention to China and the Asia-Pacific.

Current research interests

  • Female leadership and female empowerment
  • Religious and linguistic diversity and their social consequences
  • Social media control and political communication
  • Gender inequalities and implications for youth
  • Political trust, media trust, and social trust

Data & Code

When conducting quantitative research, I work with R. I enjoy the language and the supportive R community, and share practical notes and code from my own social research.

Topics include data cleaning and reshaping; regression and general linear models; network analysis; agent-based modelling; web scraping; Chinese text mining and sentiment analysis; GIS and spatiotemporal analysis; and interactive data visualization.

I also share data and programming code for reproducible research. Materials are for academic use. Please do not edit or redistribute datasets without permission, and cite the associated work when using them.

Selected code, data and slides on GitHub

Contact

咨询研究生申请、科研合作、音乐合作、发表出版等事宜，请联络我的电子邮箱：huiquanzhang [at] um.edu.mo。
