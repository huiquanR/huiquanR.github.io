Tony Huiquan Zhang

Associate Professor of Sociology

University of Macau

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Sociology at the University of Macau. I have published more than 20 research articles in internationally recognized journals, including Nature, The Leadership Quarterly (ABDC A*; ABS 4), Sociology (ABDC A*; ABS 4), Journal of Occupational Health Psychology (ABDC A; ABS 4), The British Journal of Sociology (ABDC A), Social Problems (ABDC A), and The China Quarterly (ABDC A), among others.

我是张汇泉，现任职澳门大学社会学系副教授。我的研究兴趣包括社会与政治心理学，政治文化与公众意见，比较研究（中国及亚太），互联网与社交媒体、不平等与多样性等领域。我的作品见于知名学术期刊如《Nature》（《自然》杂志）、《Leadership Quarterly》、《Sociology》、《Journal of Occupational Health Psychology》、《British Journal of Sociology》、《Social Problems》、《China Quarterly》、《the Journal of Contemporary China》、《社会学评论》、《国外社会科学》等刊物。

I am currently writing a Chinese-language book on Mandarin pop lyric writing, provisionally titled Reflections on Mandarin Pop Lyric Writing (《国语流行歌词创作随谈》). Publishers and editors interested in the project are welcome to contact me for further details.

我正在写一本有关华语流行歌词的中文书稿（初定名《国语流行歌词创作随谈》），欢迎感兴趣的出版业者及编辑垂询。

Data & Code

When conducting quantitative research, I work with R. I enjoy the language and the supportive R community, and share practical notes and code from my own social research.

Topics include data cleaning and reshaping; regression and general linear models; network analysis; agent-based modelling; web scraping; Chinese text mining and sentiment analysis; GIS and spatiotemporal analysis; and interactive data visualization.

I also share data and programming code for reproducible research. Materials are for academic use. Please do not edit or redistribute datasets without permission, and cite the associated work when using them.
